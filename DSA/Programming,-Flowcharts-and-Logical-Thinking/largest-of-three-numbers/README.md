## 📝 Problem Statement

You are given three integers **A** , **B** , and **C** . Find and print the largest among the three integers.

**Input**

- The input contains three space-separated integers A, B, and C.

**Output**

Print the largest of the three integers without labels or trailing spaces.

### Example 1:

**Input:**

```
3 7 5
```

**Output:**

```
7
```

**Explanation:**

7 is the largest among 3, 7, and 5.

### Example 2:

**Input:**

```
10 4 8
```

**Output:**

```
10
```

**Explanation:**

10 is the largest among 10, 4, and 8.
